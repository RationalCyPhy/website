Platform Overview

Autonomous systems
built to be trusted

We build powerful drones, their autonomy stacks, and the rigorous tooling to design, verify, and reason about AI-enabled systems.

Rational Cyphy develops physical intelligence platforms at the intersection of robotics, formal methods, and machine learning. Our work spans the full stack — from custom drone hardware and onboard perception systems to formal verification tools that provide provable safety guarantees for AI-enabled autonomy. Backed by NASA, AFRL, and NSF, we are based at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

§ 02 Technology Platform
[1]
RTAEval
Safety Assurance
RTAEval is a formal assurance tool for AI-enabled autonomy stacks. It enables systematic evaluation and verification of safety properties in runtime assurance (RTA) architectures — providing rigorous, certifiable guarantees for autonomous systems operating in safety-critical environments.
Formal Methods AI Safety Verification
[2]
Mapping Drone
Hardware Platform
A high-performance autonomous drone platform engineered for precision aerial mapping and environmental data acquisition. The system integrates custom sensing, onboard compute, and a full autonomy stack designed for demanding real-world operational conditions.
Hardware Autonomy Sensing
[3]
Visual Tracking & Mapping
Perception System
Real-time 3D visual tracking and environment mapping using 3D Gaussian Splatting. The system delivers high-fidelity spatial reconstruction and object tracking for onboard situational awareness in complex, dynamic scenes — enabling precise measurement and navigation.
